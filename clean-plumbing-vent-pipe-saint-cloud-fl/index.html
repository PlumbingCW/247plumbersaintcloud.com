How to Clean a Plumbing Vent Pipe in Saint Cloud, FL

A blocked vent pipe can make drains gurgle and slow down fixtures, and the cause sits on your roof. This guide explains how vents work, how to check one and when to leave the climb to a plumber.

By the Saint Cloud 24/7 Plumbing team. Updated November 12, 2026.

What a plumbing vent does

A plumbing vent is a pipe that runs from your drain system up through the roof. It lets air enter the drain pipes so water flows smoothly, and it lets sewer gas escape above the house instead of inside it.

Without air, a draining fixture pulls a vacuum behind it. The water slows, the trap can be sucked dry and a gurgle sounds as air forces its way through. If the vent is blocked, you hear the gurgle and smell the gas.

Signs of a blocked vent

These point to the vent and not just the drain:

  • Gurgling from a sink, tub or toilet when another fixture drains.
  • Slow drains in several fixtures with no clog found.
  • Sewer smell inside the house, especially near a drain.
  • A toilet that bubbles when you flush.
  • Drains that clear after you open a vent cap or run a snake, then slow again.

Compare these with a main line blockage, where the lowest drain backs up and sewage may rise. If that is happening, see our sewer line cleaning page.

What blocks a vent in Florida

Vent pipes are open at the top, so anything that lands on the roof can fall in. Leaves, palm frond bits, nests, and debris after a storm are common. Insects and small animals also nest in vents.

Occasionally the problem is inside, where a collapsed or poorly installed vent section reduces airflow. If cleaning the vent opening does not help, the plumber looks further down.

How to check and clean a vent safely

Work only on a dry day, and never go on a roof if you are unsure. Roof falls are a real hazard.

  1. Locate the vent stacks on the roof from the ground. They are usually round pipes a few inches across.
  2. Use a ladder with a spotter and wear shoes with good grip, or ask a pro to do the roof portion.
  3. Look into the vent opening with a flashlight and remove visible debris by hand with gloves.
  4. Run a garden hose down the vent and watch whether water drains away. If it backs up, the blockage is below.
  5. If the hose fills the pipe, use a drain snake rated for the pipe size to break up the blockage, then rinse again.
  6. Test the drains inside the house after cleaning.

Do not use pressure washing on a vent, and do not poke a rigid object into a pipe you cannot see. You may damage a joint.

Vent problem or drain problem

These patterns help separate the two.

A dry trap can also cause smells. Pour a gallon of water into a seldom-used drain to refill it.

When to call a plumber

Call if you cannot reach the roof, if the hose test shows a full blockage, if the problem returns or if you smell sewer gas that does not go away. A plumber can use a camera and a longer snake to clear the vent and check the rest of the system.

If the symptoms suggest the main line, hydro jetting may be the right fix. If you smell a strong sewer odor and feel ill, open windows and leave the house, then call a plumber.

Saint Cloud local note: what the county says about this work

The county permit page does not require a permit for clearing sewer blockages, and cleaning out a vent or a drain line falls in that spirit. If a plumber finds a damaged section of vent that needs replacing, ask the building department for your address whether a permit applies.

The page adds that regulated trades like plumbing call for licensed subcontractors when an owner acts as the builder. For anything past cleaning, hire a licensed plumber and verify the license at myfloridalicense.com.

Quick answers for Saint Cloud homeowners

Can I clean a plumbing vent myself?

A homeowner can clear visible debris from the opening if it is safe to reach. Roof work carries a fall risk, so many homeowners hire a plumber for it.

How do I know which pipe on the roof is the vent?

Vent stacks are round pipes extending a few inches above the roof surface, often near the bathrooms or kitchen. A plumber can identify each one.

Does a blocked vent cause sewage backup?

Usually it causes slow drains and gurgling. A main line blockage causes a backup, though the two can occur together.

Does cleaning a vent in Osceola County require a permit?

Osceola County's permit page lists clearing sewer blockages as not requiring a permit. If a vent section must be replaced, ask the building department that covers your address.

Why does my house smell like sewer after a storm?

Debris in a vent, a dry trap or a loose connection can let sewer gas into the house. Pour water into unused drains and call a plumber if it continues.
