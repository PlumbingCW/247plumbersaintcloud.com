Saint Cloud and its ZIP codes

Our base at 4125 Neptune Road sits in ZIP code 34769. We also serve 34771 to the east, 34772 to the south and 34773 toward Harmony. Across all four, drain cleaning, water heater repair and leak detection are the most common calls.

Within Saint Cloud, the places residents use to describe where they live include Neptune Road, Narcoossee Road, Kissimmee Park Road and the lakefront on East Lake Tohopekaliga. Tell the dispatcher the cross street and the plumber plans the route.

Kissimmee

Kissimmee is the Osceola County seat, about 10 road miles northwest of our base by an estimate. Our Kissimmee page covers its ZIP codes, vacation rentals, the resort corridor and older downtown buildings.

Harmony

Harmony is a master-planned community southeast of Saint Cloud with ZIP code 34773 and homes from the early 2000s onward. Our Harmony page covers water heaters at the end of their life, builder-grade fixtures and leak detection.

Nearby communities within reach

Poinciana, to the south of Kissimmee, and Narcoossee, to the north of Saint Cloud, are within our dispatch area. They do not have separate pages, but the same 24/7 line serves them. Tell the dispatcher your address and the plumber is booked.

If your address is outside the towns listed, call anyway. The dispatcher confirms what is reachable, and some nearby addresses are covered that do not appear here.

How the route is planned

When a call comes in, the dispatcher looks at the address, the type of problem and the schedule, and books the first open appointment. For most addresses in the four Saint Cloud ZIP codes, the trip from Neptune Road is short. For addresses across the lake, the plumber plans the route through the main roads and tells you the plan when the appointment is set.

Traffic and weather can change a route, especially during heavy summer storms. The dispatcher is honest about what is realistic, and we do not promise an arrival time we cannot keep.

Newer neighborhoods and older streets

The towns we serve are a mix of ages, and the age of a house shapes the calls. Older streets in Saint Cloud and downtown Kissimmee tend to have older drain pipe and older fixtures, so backups and slow drains come up often. Newer subdivisions in Harmony and the growing parts of Saint Cloud more often see water heaters reaching the end of their life and fixtures that wore out faster than expected.

Tell the dispatcher the rough age of your home when you call. A plumber who knows the pipe material and the heater type before arriving brings the right parts and saves a second trip.

Whatever the area, the same steps apply in an emergency. Close the main water valve, stay out of standing water near power and call the 24/7 line at (321) 463-9969.

Questions to ask before you book

If you live near the edge of a service area, a few questions help. Is the address inside the dispatch area? Is there a gate code or an access issue? Is anyone home, or does a neighbor have a key?

Landlords and property managers should also say who approves the work and how the plumber reaches that person. A decision that has to wait for an owner can add hours to a repair, and a dispatcher who knows about it in advance can plan around it.

Water, permits and who to ask

Water in these towns comes through Toho Water Authority, whose service area page lists Saint Cloud, Kissimmee and Poinciana. If a leak is on the utility's side of the meter, the utility is the one to call. If it is on the house side, it is yours to repair, and a plumber can tell you which side you are on.

Permits depend on the work and the address. Osceola County's permit page lists water heaters, replacement fixtures and re-piping as permit-required, and lists clearing sewer blockages and repairing leaks as not. Inside a city, the city building office may run its own process, so ask the office that covers your address before work starts.

Plumbing in every one of these towns follows the standards of the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, whichever office issues the permit.

Neighborhood pages in Saint Cloud

Four parts of Saint Cloud have their own pages, because their housing and plumbing questions differ. Downtown Saint Cloud covers the original 1909 street grid by the lake. Weslyn Park and Sunbridge covers newer builder-warranty homes. Crossprairie covers the growing master plan along Cross Prairie Parkway. Twin Lakes covers the 55-plus community.

If your street is not on that list, call (321) 463-9969 anyway. The dispatcher confirms what is reachable.
