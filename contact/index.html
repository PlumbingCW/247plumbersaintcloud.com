What to tell the dispatcher

A few details help the plumber arrive ready:

  • The address and the nearest cross street.
  • What is happening, and which fixtures or areas are affected.
  • What you have already shut off.
  • The age of the home, if you know it.
  • Whether anyone will be home, and when the building is open if it is a business.
  • Anything that raises safety concerns, such as a gas smell or water near electrical panels.

If you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 first.

Planned work and business customers

You do not need an emergency to call. Water heater upgrades, fixture installs, repiping and water treatment can all be scheduled, and the dispatcher books a time that suits you.

Business customers can call the same number. Tell the dispatcher what kind of business it is, when the building is open and who can give access. Restaurants, shops and offices have different needs after hours, and the plumber plans around them.

If you are comparing options, you can ask what a visit would involve and what the price depends on. The dispatcher explains the process, and the plumber gives a clear price before work starts.

Before you call: a two-minute check

If water is leaking, close the main valve first. If a drain is backing up, stop running water. If the heater has no hot water, check whether the breaker has tripped or the pilot has gone out, and do not force anything that does not move easily.

Have your address ready, and look for the cross street if you can. Note which fixtures are affected and when the problem began. A few facts give the plumber a head start, and they shorten the call.

Hours and response

The line is staffed 24/7. A dispatcher books the first open appointment, and same-day times are available when the schedule allows. We do not promise an arrival time, and the dispatcher tells you what is realistic when you call.

You receive a clear price before the work starts. If you want to prepare, read the services page, or see which of our service areas covers you.
