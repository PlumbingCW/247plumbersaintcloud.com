About Saint Cloud 24/7 Plumbing

Saint Cloud 24/7 Plumbing is a call line that connects homes and businesses in Saint Cloud, Kissimmee and Harmony with plumbing help by phone.

Call (321) 463-9969, open 24/7

What we do

When you call (321) 463-9969, a dispatcher answers, asks what is happening and books the first open appointment. The plumber who arrives explains the problem and gives a clear price before starting. That is the whole model, and it is designed to be simple when you are stressed.

We cover drains and sewers, water heaters, leaks, fixtures, repiping and commercial work. The full list is on the services page.

How we work with plumbers

We do not publish a license number for a single contractor, because the plumber assigned to your job depends on the work and the schedule. Florida requires plumbing work to be done by a licensed plumber, so ask the plumber to show you their license when they arrive and check it at myfloridalicense.com, the state's license lookup.

The plumber assigned to your job handles the diagnosis, the price and the work. If you have a question about the job, ask the plumber at the door or call the line.

The standards that apply to the work come from the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, and Chapter 489 of the Florida Statutes covers contractor licensing.

What we do not do

This site covers plumbing. We do not pump septic tanks, repair well pumps or do electrical and air conditioning work, and a gas leak is a call to the gas utility or 911 before it is a call to us. We would rather say so than let you wait for a service we do not provide.

Where a job depends on a permit, we tell you, and we point you to the building department that covers your address. Osceola County's permit page is a good starting point for what needs one.

How a job runs from call to finish

The call comes first, and the dispatcher books an appointment. The plumber arrives, looks at the problem and explains what is wrong in plain terms. You get a clear price before work starts, and you decide whether to proceed.

During the work, the plumber keeps the area as clean as the job allows and tells you if anything unexpected turns up. If the scope grows, the price is updated before new work begins. When the job is done, the plumber tests the repair, shows you what was done and answers your questions.

That is the whole process. It is meant to be easy to follow when you are stressed, and it does not depend on technical knowledge.

Who we serve

Most calls come from homeowners, but landlords, property managers and small businesses call too. A rental that sits empty between guests, a restaurant with a backed-up kitchen line and a shop with a failed water heater all need a plumber quickly, and the same 24/7 line serves them.

If you manage several properties, tell the dispatcher which one has the problem and who can give access. Clear details about access and contacts keep the appointment moving.

We also take planned work such as fixture installs, water heater upgrades and repiping, scheduled at a time that suits you.

What a good plumbing visit looks like

A good visit starts before the plumber arrives, with a dispatcher who has written down the address, the symptoms and what you have already done. It continues with a plumber who introduces themselves, listens to your account and then looks for themselves instead of assuming.

The explanation should be short and plain. What failed, why it failed, what the options are and what each costs. You should hear the repair option and the replacement option when both exist, and you should be free to ask for time to think when the situation is not an emergency.

After the work, the plumber should test what was fixed, point out anything that will need attention later and leave the area as tidy as the job allows. If something looks wrong a day or two later, call back and say so. A plumbing repair should hold, and a problem that returns deserves a second look.

Those expectations are simple, but they are the ones people most often tell us were missing from a past experience. We built the process around them.
