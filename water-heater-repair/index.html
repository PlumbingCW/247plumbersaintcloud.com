What water heater repair covers

Water heater repair is the diagnosis and fixing of a failed part on an electric, gas or hybrid tank water heater so it makes hot water safely again. The common repairs are heating elements, thermostats, thermocouples, igniters, gas control valves, relief valves, drain valves and supply connections.

Many problems are small and fixable. A tank that is leaking from its body is a different matter, because a rusted-through steel tank cannot be patched, and that situation is covered in our replacement guide.

When a water heater problem is an emergency

Call at any hour if the tank is leaking, if water is running from the relief valve pipe, if the unit is hot to the touch with no hot water, or if you smell gas. The dispatch line is answered 24/7, including nights and weekends, and a dispatcher books the first open appointment.

Do these three things while you wait. Close the cold water shutoff valve on top of the tank, turn the breaker off for an electric unit or set a gas unit's control to off, and move belongings away from the water.

If you smell gas, leave the house, do not touch switches, and call the gas utility or 911 from outside before you call us. A plumber comes after the area is safe.

Symptoms and what they usually mean

These are the most common complaints and the likely causes behind them:

  • No hot water at all: tripped breaker or high-limit switch, failed element or thermostat on an electric unit, or a pilot that will not stay lit on a gas unit.
  • Water warm but not hot: one failed element, a broken dip tube mixing cold water in, or a thermostat set too low.
  • Popping or rumbling: sediment on the tank bottom that traps steam under it.
  • Rust-colored hot water: the tank interior or the anode rod is corroding.
  • A sulfur or rotten egg smell in hot water only: bacteria reacting with the anode rod, a fix that is often simple.
  • Water around the base: a loose fitting, a leaking relief valve or a failed tank.

How a plumber diagnoses the problem

The first step is to find out whether the unit is electric, gas or a hybrid heat pump model. A multimeter checks elements, thermostats and voltage on electric units. On a gas unit the plumber checks the pilot assembly, the thermocouple and the gas control valve and tests the flue draft.

A leak hunt follows. Many leaks are not the tank. Supply connections, the relief valve, the drain valve and a condensation drip from the cold pipe can all mimic a tank leak, and a dry paper towel pressed to each joint finds the source.

If the tank itself is leaking, the plumber says so plainly. A repair would not last, and the honest recommendation is to replace the unit.

Why Saint Cloud water heaters wear the way they do

The City of Saint Cloud's water comes from the Floridan aquifer through Toho Water Authority. The utility's annual report says the raw water has hydrogen sulfide, the rotten egg gas, which is removed by aeration before disinfection. That is worth knowing if you smell sulfur from hot taps only, because the cause is usually the heater's anode rod and not the supply.

The same report does not list a hardness figure, so test your tap or ask Toho for its water quality information. If scale is a concern, flushing a tank heater once a year slows sediment buildup, and the plumber can tell you what the drain water looks like.

Many local heaters sit in garages, so a leak can reach stored items before anyone notices. A drain pan with a line to the outside, installed under the tank, gives you time to react.

What decides the price of a water heater repair

A repair quote depends on these facts, and we give you a price before work starts:

  • The failed part. An element or a thermocouple is a smaller job than a gas control valve.
  • Heater type. Electric, gas and hybrid models use different parts.
  • Age and condition. A repair on a unit near the end of its life may not be worth the money.
  • Access. A heater in a tight closet or an attic takes longer to service than one in an open garage.
  • Parts on hand. A common part may be installed the same visit, and a special order takes longer.

Home insurance often covers sudden water damage caused by a burst tank but not the cost of repairing the heater itself. For your real price, call (321) 463-9969 and give us the make and age if you know them.

Standards for heater repairs

Gas water heaters are built to the ANSI Z21.10.1 standard, and the gas supply side follows NFPA 54, the National Fuel Gas Code. Electric repairs involve the NEC for the wiring, and a plumber follows the manufacturer's guidelines for every replacement part.

Questions we hear from Saint Cloud callers

Do you handle water heater repair emergencies at night or on weekends in Saint Cloud?

Yes. The line is answered 24 hours a day, and a dispatcher books the first open appointment for a leaking or failed heater. Shut off the cold supply and the power or gas before you wait.

Why does my hot water smell like rotten eggs?

In many cases bacteria in the tank react with the anode rod and produce hydrogen sulfide. If the smell is only in the hot water, the heater is the likely source. A plumber can flush the tank and replace or swap the anode rod.

Can a leaking water heater be repaired?

It depends where it leaks. A loose fitting or a bad relief valve is repairable. A leak from the tank body is not, because the steel has rusted through, and the unit needs to be replaced.

Why is my water heater making popping noises?

Sediment on the tank bottom traps water that flashes to steam and pops. Flushing the tank can reduce it, though heavy buildup may mean the tank is near the end of its life.

Is it safe to keep using a heater that leaks slowly?

A slow leak can become a flood, and it can damage drywall, flooring and stored items. Turn off the cold water supply and the power or gas, and call a plumber the same day.
