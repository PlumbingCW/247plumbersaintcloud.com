What a garbage disposal repair covers

Garbage disposal repair fixes a jammed, humming, leaking or noisy disposal, or replaces it when the motor or housing is worn out. Installation sets a new disposal under the sink, wires it, connects it to the drain and the dishwasher line, and tests it.

The disposal is a grinder, not a trash can. It chops food into small pieces so water carries them down the drain. Most problems come from what goes into it and how long it has been in service.

When a disposal problem is an emergency

Call at any hour if water is leaking from the disposal body onto the cabinet floor, if the sink is full of standing water and will not drain, or if the unit smells burnt. The line is answered 24/7, including nights and weekends.

Turn off the disposal switch and the breaker if you plan to look at it. Never reach into the grinder chamber. If water is leaking, close the angle stops under the sink and put a bowl or towels under the leak.

Same-day appointments are offered when available, and a clear price comes before work begins.

A disposal that has been jamming repeatedly, leaking from the body or running loud for months is usually near the end of its life, and replacement costs less than another repair.

What should not go down a disposal

A disposal does not handle everything. Keep these out:

  • Grease, fat and oil, which coat the pipe and harden downstream.
  • Fibrous foods such as celery stalks, corn husks and artichokes.
  • Coffee grounds in large amounts, which pack into the trap.
  • Bones, fruit pits and shells.
  • Pasta, rice and potato peels, which swell and gum up the line.
  • Non-food items, including utensils and bottle caps.

Run cold water before, during and after use for about fifteen seconds. Cold water keeps fats solid so they flush through and do not coat the pipe.

Installing a new garbage disposal

A basic swap takes a couple of hours. The old unit is disconnected from the drain, the dishwasher hose and the power, then unmounted from the sink flange. The new one hangs from the same mount, and the plumber reconnects the discharge, the dishwasher knockout plug and the electrical supply.

The dishwasher connection matters. A new disposal has a knockout plug that must be removed before the dishwasher hose is attached, and a high loop or an air gap keeps sink water from running back into the dishwasher. Skipping the plug removal is the most common installation error.

Hardwired or plug-in power depends on what the kitchen already has. If the kitchen has no switch for a disposal, an electrician may be needed for the wiring. Osceola County's permit page lists new and replacement fixtures among permit-required plumbing work, so ask the building department that covers your address.

Septic systems and disposals

Most Saint Cloud homes connect to the city sewer, though some properties outside the city and in rural parts of the county use a septic tank. A disposal adds solids to the tank and can shorten the time between pump-outs. If your home is on septic, ask before adding a disposal.

If a disposal clog turns out to be in the main line and not the sink trap, a plumber can switch to sewer line cleaning.

What decides the price of a disposal job

We give you a price before work starts:

  • Repair or replace. A reset or a flange reseal is smaller than a new unit.
  • Horsepower. A third-horsepower unit costs less than three-quarters horsepower.

Codes for the sink connection

A disposal ties into the drain under the Florida Building Code, Plumbing and into power under the NEC, and the manufacturer's guidelines cover the dishwasher connection. If the job needs new wiring or a changed drain, hire a licensed professional.

Questions we hear from Saint Cloud callers

Do you handle garbage disposal emergencies at night or on weekends in Saint Cloud?

Yes. The line is answered 24 hours a day. Turn off the disposal, close the angle stops if it leaks and call, and we book the first open appointment.

How do I clear a jammed disposal?

Switch it off at the wall. From below, insert a hex key in the center socket and turn it back and forth to free the flywheel. Then press the red reset button on the bottom and test it with cold water running.

Why does my disposal smell?

Food residue clings to the grinding chamber and rots. Cold water, a little dish soap and a few citrus peels or ice cubes help. If the smell persists, the trap or branch line may be clogged.

How long does a disposal last?

Many last several years under ordinary home use, depending on the model, the horsepower and what goes into it. A leak from the body is the clearest sign it is time to replace.

Is it easy to install a disposal myself?

It is manageable for some people, but the dishwasher knockout plug, the mount and the wiring are easy to get wrong. Plumbing is a regulated trade in Florida, so confirm the rules with the building department.
