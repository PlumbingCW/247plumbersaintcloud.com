Plumbing help for a newer Sunbridge home

Sunbridge is a master-planned community developed by Tavistock Development Company across Osceola and Orange counties and built out in phases over many years. Weslyn Park is its first residential neighborhood on the Osceola side, with first homes offered for sale in 2021 according to a local real estate site. Builders named in sales material include David Weekley, Ashton Woods, Taylor Morrison, Toll Brothers, Pulte and Dream Finders.

A house this new has different plumbing questions than a 1950s ranch. The pipes are young, the fixtures are builder grade or recent upgrades, and the first question is often who is responsible for the repair. If something is wrong right now, call. If it can wait a day, read the warranty section below first.

Where Weslyn Park and Sunbridge sit

Plain facts first, so you know what this page claims.

Location facts

Sunbridge is in northern Osceola County, and Weslyn Park is off Cyrils Drive with ZIP code 34771. A Saint Cloud mailing address does not by itself show whether a parcel is inside the City of Saint Cloud, so check the Osceola County Property Appraiser if that matters to you.

Water and sewer service is listed as Toho Water Authority, and a separate 55-plus community, Del Webb Sunbridge, shares the master plan. Both a homeowners association and a community development district assessment can apply to Weslyn Park homes, so read your tax bill and closing documents.

Builder warranty first, plumber second

New homes usually come with a builder warranty, and many have a separate structural or systems period. A leak behind a wall or a failed fixture in the first year may be the builder's job to fix. Call the builder's warranty line, take photos and note the date. A repair done by someone else first can complicate a claim.

Some problems cannot wait for a warranty visit. A supply line spraying water, a water heater leaking onto a floor or a toilet overflowing is an emergency regardless of who pays. Shut off the water, document the damage and call the plumber. Tell the dispatcher the house is under warranty so the invoice is itemized clearly for your claim.

What newer-home plumbing calls tend to involve

These are general patterns for recently built homes and not findings about any street in Sunbridge.

Water heaters

Tank and tankless units are both common in new construction. Repairs and flushes keep a unit running, and tankless service needs descaling on a schedule.

Fixtures and supply lines

Faucets, toilet fill valves and flexible supply lines are the parts most often replaced in a young house. Faucet repair and toilet repair are quick visits.

Water quality

Toho Water Authority's report for its Saint Cloud system describes aquifer water treated with ion exchange, aeration and chlorine, with no hardness figure we could find. A softener or filter is a personal choice after you test.

Add-ons after move-in

Homeowners often add a garbage disposal, a filtration system or an extra hose connection after closing. Plumbing installation covers those.

If you find water you did not expect

Newer homes are tightly built, so water can hide behind drywall or under cabinets.

  1. Close the main water valve and the fixture shutoff if you can identify the source.
  2. Photograph the damage and any wet material before you move anything.
  3. Call your builder's warranty line if the home is within its warranty period.
  4. Call (321) 463-9969 if the water is active, if you cannot reach the builder or if the problem is not covered.

Permits and licensing

Osceola County's permit page lists water heaters, replacement fixtures and re-piping as permit-required plumbing work, and clearing blockages and repairing leaks as not requiring a permit. If your parcel is outside a city, the county handles permits, and if it is inside, the city may. Ask the office that covers your address.

Florida requires plumbing work to be done by a licensed plumber. Ask to see the license and look it up at myfloridalicense.com before work starts.

Plumbing questions from Sunbridge and Weslyn Park homeowners

Is Weslyn Park inside Saint Cloud city limits?

We could not confirm that from public sources. The neighborhood has Saint Cloud mailing addresses in ZIP code 34771. The Osceola County Property Appraiser can tell you the jurisdiction for your parcel.

Should I call the builder or a plumber first?

Call the builder first for a non-urgent problem in a home under warranty. For an active leak or overflow, stop the water first and then decide who to call.

Do new homes have hard water?

We did not find a hardness figure in the Toho Water Authority report for the Saint Cloud system. Test your tap if scale or spotting is a concern.

Does Del Webb Sunbridge use the same plumbing service?

A plumber can service the 55-plus community too. Its rules and fees differ from Weslyn Park, so check with your association.

What does a water heater replacement need?

Osceola County's permit page lists water heaters as permit-required, and the work needs a licensed plumber.
