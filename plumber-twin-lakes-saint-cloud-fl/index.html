Plumbing help for an active-adult community

Twin Lakes is a 55-plus neighborhood in Saint Cloud. Streets named in listings include Twin Lakes Boulevard, Fountainwood Drive, Southland Street, Datura Loop and Meadowedge Loop. A real estate directory credits Jones Homes and Del Webb as builders and notes that both a homeowners association and a community development district apply.

Residents of an age-restricted community tend to ask different questions than a young family does. Comfort, safety and low maintenance come first. Call for a repair, or read on to plan an upgrade that makes the bathrooms easier to use.

The facts below are drawn from more than one source, and we say where a point could not be confirmed.

Local news reported that the Saint Cloud city council approved the annexation of nearly 1,000 acres across Kings Crest, Edgewater and Twin Lakes, and it described Twin Lakes as a 55-plus community. We did not find the finished annexation date, so ask the city if your jurisdiction matters.

Plumbing upgrades that suit the way people live here

These are common choices in active-adult homes and not claims about any home in Twin Lakes.

Comfort-height toilets

A toilet that sits taller is easier to get up from. Toilet installation covers the swap and a new wax ring.

Lever faucets

Lever handles are easier on the hands than round knobs. Faucet repair and replacement is usually a short visit.

Scald protection

A water heater set too hot can burn skin quickly. A plumber can check the temperature setting and the relief valve during water heater service.

Reachable shutoffs

Know where the main shutoff and each fixture valve are, and make sure you can turn them without tools.

If you spend part of the year away

Some owners in a 55-plus community travel for weeks at a time. A house that is empty can leak for days before anyone notices. If that describes you, ask a neighbor or a property manager to check the house, close the main water valve before you leave and keep the water heater on a safe setting.

A small leak sensor under the water heater or beside the toilet sends an alert before a small drip becomes damage. For a bill that jumped while you were gone, leak detection locates a hidden leak without tearing up the floor.

When something goes wrong at home

Stay safe first. Water and electricity do not mix.

  1. Close the main water valve. Know where it is before you need it.
  2. Turn off power to the wet area if you can reach the breaker safely, and stay out of standing water.
  3. Ask a family member or neighbor to help if lifting or kneeling is hard.
  4. Call (321) 463-9969 and tell the dispatcher your street, what you shut off and whether anyone needs help getting to the shutoff.

Making a bathroom easier to use

Many owners start with the toilet and the shower. A taller toilet bowl, a handheld shower head on a slide bar and a pressure-balancing or thermostatic shower valve are all plumbing choices that change daily comfort. A pressure-balancing valve helps keep the temperature steady when someone flushes or runs a tap elsewhere in the house.

Changing a shower valve means opening the wall, so plan it with any tile work. A plumber can tell you what the existing valve is and whether a new trim will fit or the valve body needs replacing. Grab bars are a carpentry and tile matter, but the plumber can mark where the supply lines run so the blocking goes in safely.

Permits, codes and license

Osceola County's permit page lists water heaters, replacement fixtures and re-piping as permit-required plumbing work, and clearing blockages and repairing leaks as not requiring a permit. Ask the office that covers your address. Water heater safety devices follow the Florida Building Code, Plumbing.

Florida requires plumbing work to be done by a licensed plumber. Look up any license at myfloridalicense.com, and check your association rules before changing anything visible from the street.

Plumbing questions from Twin Lakes homeowners

Is Twin Lakes only for residents 55 and older?

Real estate directories describe it as a 55-plus community. Your association can confirm the rules for your household.

Is Twin Lakes inside Saint Cloud?

It has Saint Cloud addresses, and local news reported the city council approved annexing it. We did not find the final date, so ask the city if the jurisdiction matters to you.

Can a plumber install a comfort-height toilet?

Yes. It is a routine installation and often takes one visit.

What should I do before leaving for a long trip?

Close the main water valve, set the water heater to a safe setting and have someone check the house.

Do I need permission from the association?

Changes visible from outside may need approval. Interior plumbing repairs usually do not, but check your documents.
